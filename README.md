# Sales_DashBoard


# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that turns raw sales data into clear, decision-ready insights on **sales, profit, units sold, customer segments, products and countries**.

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard.png)

---

## 🎯 Objective

To help a business answer simple but important questions:

- How much are we selling and earning?
- Which customer segment brings the most revenue?
- Which products and countries perform best?
- How does profit change over time?

---

## 📈 Key Metrics (Sep 2013 – Nov 2014)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Gross Sales | 115.42M |
| Sales (after discounts) | 106.73M |
| Profit | 14.87M |
| Units Sold | 1.02M |

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Government** is the top segment with **46.58M (43.65%)** of total sales.
- **Small Business** follows with **39.01M (36.55%)**, then **Enterprise** with **17.31M (16.22%)**.
- Midmarket and Channel Partners together contribute only a small share, which is room for growth.
- Discounts reduced gross sales by about **8.7M (~7.5%)**.
- Profit margin is roughly **13.9%** (14.87M / 106.73M).
- Monthly profit is **seasonal**, with peaks of about **1.78M** and dips near **0.60M**.

---

## 🧩 Dashboard Visuals

- **KPI Cards:** Gross Sales, Sales, Profit, Units Sold
- **Date Slicer:** filter the full report by time period
- **Bar Chart:** Sales by Segment
- **Donut Chart:** Sales share by Segment
- **Line Chart:** Profit trend by Year, Quarter, Month and Day
- **Map:** Sales by Country
- **Stacked Bar Chart:** Sales by Product and Segment (Paseo, VTT, Velo)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

- **Power BI Desktop**: data modeling, visuals, slicers
- **DAX**: measures for Sales, Profit and Units
- **Power Query**: data cleaning and transformation
- **Data Storytelling**: dashboard layout and design

## 🚀 How to Use

2. Open it in **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the **date slicer** and click on any visual to filter the whole report.
